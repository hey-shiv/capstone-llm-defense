Can a small model keep a secret?

One word, CAPSTONE, is hidden in the assistant’s instructions. Send a prompt, benign or hostile, to three versions of the same 0.5B model and see who leaks it and who blocks harmless requests.

Everything runs on this machine. Replies are generated live from the trained adapters, then checked by a detector that also catches the secret spelled backwards, in leetspeak, in base64 or in ROT13.
